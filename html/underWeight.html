Exercises For Under Weight

Explore these exercises specifically designed for individuals looking to gain weight and build muscle mass.

Exercise 1: Squats

Squats are great for building lower body strength. Perform them with weights for added resistance.

Exercise 2: Deadlifts

Deadlifts help in developing the back and overall body strength. Start with lighter weights and gradually increase.

Exercise 3: Bench Press

Bench presses are excellent for chest and arm development. Use proper form to avoid injuries.

Exercise 4: Pull-Ups

Pull-ups are a bodyweight exercise that helps build upper body strength. Use assistance if needed.
